Sports Court Surfacing & Timber Flooring in Singapore

H&H Resources provides court systems for schools, clubs and public sports facilities in Singapore. Our portfolio includes acrylic court surfacing at Republic Polytechnic and Tanah Merah Country Club, and timber sports flooring at Ngee Ann Polytechnic.

  • Timber sports flooring
