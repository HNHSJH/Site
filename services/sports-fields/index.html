Sports Field & Artificial Turf Construction in Singapore

H&H Resources works on sports fields for schools, clubs and government facilities in Singapore. Our expertise spans artificial, natural and hybrid football fields, with artificial turf references at Our Tampines Hub and Ngee Ann Polytechnic.

Scope of work

  • Artificial football field systems
  • Natural football field systems
  • Hybrid football field systems
