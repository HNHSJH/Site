Built on experience. Made for play.

With roots dating back to 1980 under M S Construction Pte Ltd, H&H Resources brings decades of hands-on experience to sports field, court, turf and landscape construction for schools, clubs and government agencies in Singapore.

Our track record spans more than 100 fields, with project references including artificial turf at Our Tampines Hub and Ngee Ann Polytechnic, and sports-court surfacing at Tanah Merah Country Club.

At Our Tampines Hub, our work on the artificial-turf football field forms part of our contribution to Singapore’s community sports spaces.

From ground preparation and drainage to turf installation and specialist finishes, we bring skilled site teams, dedicated machinery and practical construction knowledge to every project.
